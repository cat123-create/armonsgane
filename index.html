Welcome, miner!

1. Open the Mines tab and send a worker to a mine. The trip takes a few seconds, then your worker comes back with credits.

2. Each mine fits up to 5 workers. Hire more workers, and upgrade Mines at once to work several different mines together. The Workers tab shows what each one is doing.

3. Spend credits in the Shop. Coins buy upgrades, speed, workers, turrets, and new mines.

4. Every 100 energy you spend, a puzzle appears. Solve it to keep going, or pay energy, credits, or coins to skip it.

5. In the Auto tab, buy an Auto-Dispatcher and autobuyers that spend your money for you.
