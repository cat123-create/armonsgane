Welcome, miner!

1. Open the Mines tab and send a worker to a mine. The trip takes a few seconds, then your worker comes back with credits.

2. Each mine fits up to 5 workers. Hire more workers, and upgrade Mines at once to work several different mines together. The Workers tab shows what each one is doing.

3. Spend credits in the Shop. Coins buy upgrades, speed, workers, turrets, and new mines.

4. Every 750 energy you spend, a puzzle appears. You must solve it to keep going, and you get +20 energy for it. You can make puzzles rarer in Upgrades.

5. In the Auto tab, buy an Auto-Dispatcher and autobuyers that spend your money for you.

6. The last page of Mines holds 10 special caves. They are gambles: big rewards, or thieves who steal from you. Each one runs alone, with no other mines at the same time, and costs coins to enter.

Log in

Your username and password can be anything you like. You do not need an email. Just remember them, because there is no password reset.

Log in once and this device stays signed in and saves automatically until you tap Log out.

Progress does not sync by itself between devices. On your other device, open the Stats tab, tap Make save code, then paste it here. Your progress and password come with it.

Prestige

Offline earnings

When you close the game or switch away, your turrets and Auto-Dispatcher keep working at 10% speed. They stop the moment they would reach a puzzle, or when they run out of energy, so the game is never fully AFK. A welcome-back card tells you what they earned.
